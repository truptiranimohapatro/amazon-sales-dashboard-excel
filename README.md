## 🎯 Project Overview

This project analyzes Amazon sales data for **2023 and 2024** in a single, filter-driven Excel dashboard. Users can slice the data by month, category, country, and payment method to explore revenue, order behavior, and customer trends.

## 📈 Dashboard Features

- 🎛️ **Interactive slicers:** Month, Category, Country, Payment Method
- 📅 **Monthly sales trend:** 2023 vs 2024 comparison
- 🗂️ **Category view:** Sales by product category
- 💳 **Payment methods:** Amazon Pay, Cash on Delivery, Credit Card, Debit Card, Net Banking, UPI
- 🏆 **Top 10 products:** Best-selling products by sales
- 📦 **Order distribution:** Delivered, Shipped, Pending, Returned, Cancelled
- 🌍 **Country view:** Sales by country

## 🧹 Data Cleaning

- Removed **duplicate records** from the raw sales data
- Handled **missing values** in key columns
- Standardized **text fields** (category, country, payment method) using `TRIM` and `PROPER`
- Verified data types for **date, quantity, and amount** columns
- Checked for **zero or unusual values** in order amount

## 🔍 Key Insights

**Overall**
- The dashboard covers **39,966 orders** from **27,484 customers**, which is about **1.45 orders per customer**.
- Total revenue is **39.4M**, with an average order value of about **985.90**.
- Both the **cancellation rate and return rate are 3%**, so most orders complete successfully.

**Monthly Trend**
- Monthly sales stay in a band of roughly **1.5M to 1.75M** across both years.
- **May 2024 is the peak month** (about 1.75M), while **February 2023 is the lowest** (about 1.49M).
- 2023 and 2024 move close together, with no strong seasonal spike in either year.

**Category**
- **Electronics** is the top category, followed closely by **Books**.
- **Clothing** has the lowest sales among the six categories.
- Category sales are fairly close to each other, so revenue is spread evenly.

**Payment Method**
- **Credit Card** is the most used payment method.
- **Cash on Delivery** has the smallest share.

**Country**
- The **United States** leads by a wide margin, contributing roughly **70%** of total sales.
- **India** is the second-largest market, followed by Canada, the United Kingdom, and Australia.

**Products**
- The top 10 products sit in a tight range of about **827K to 864K** in sales, with no single product dominating.
